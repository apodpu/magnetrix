Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short: Magnet Workshop does not collect personal data from you. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we don’t track you. Your game data stays on your device.

1. Who is this policy for?

This policy applies to the “Magnet Workshop” iOS app (the “App”) developed by [DEVELOPER NAME]. You can reach us at apodpu@gmail.com

3. iCloud sync (optional)

If iCloud is turned on on your device, your game progress (best move counts and tutorial status) may sync across your devices through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. This data is kept in Apple’s services under your Apple ID; as the developer we cannot access it. Your coin balance and purchase status are not synced through iCloud. You can turn syncing off in your device’s iCloud settings.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example the Full version) are made and processed through Apple’s App Store. Your payment details, such as card information, never reach us. To restore a purchase you use the “Restore purchases” button in the App; Apple verifies it through your Apple ID. As the developer we may receive aggregated sales reports from Apple; they contain no information that identifies you.

5. Third parties

The App contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or social media SDKs. Your data is not shared with or sold to anyone for advertising. The App relies only on Apple services (the App Store, in-app purchase, and optional iCloud).

If you have turned on an analytics-sharing setting such as “Share with App Developers” on your device, Apple may provide developers with aggregated, anonymous crash or usage statistics. This is governed by Apple’s own policies and can be turned off in your settings.

6. Children

The App does not collect, and does not knowingly collect, personal data from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising and no external links.

7. Retention and deletion

Deleting the App from your device deletes all of its local data on that device. To delete progress data synced through iCloud, use Settings > your Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage on your device.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data linked to you, we hold no records for you to request access to, correct or delete. You can still write to us with any privacy questions or requests, and your rights under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR in Europe and the KVKK in Türkiye) remain unaffected.

9. Changes

If we add new features to the App such as accounts, an online balance, advertising or analytics, we will update this policy and publish the change on this page together with its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app update notes.
