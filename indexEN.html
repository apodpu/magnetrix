How to play

You’ll learn it in three steps. The first levels guide you along the way.

  1. Walk and turnWalk with the arrow pad or by swiping. Use “Turn” mode to change direction without walking.
  2. Pull or pushThe magnet pulls the first crate in front of you toward you, or pushes it away. Crates slide until they hit an obstacle.
  3. Fill the platesWhen every plate holds a crate, the gate opens. Reach the exit; finish within par to earn 3 stars.

Features

Simple rules, deep puzzles.

Polar crates

Blue crates can only be pulled, red crates can only be pushed. Plan the order carefully.

Hazards

Spikes, orange patrols that move one tile per action, and arrow tiles you can only cross in one direction.

Room to make mistakes

Unlimited undo and restart. Pushing the wrong crate never ends the game.

Hint system

When you’re stuck, it shows the first move of the shortest path from where you stand. One free hint a day; more hints cost coins.

Stars and par

Every level has a par move count. Can you find the perfect solution?

Landscape, full screen

Built for touch: arrow pad, swipe, big Pull and Push buttons, haptics and sound.

Every level was verified with a computer solver: levels are solvable, the exit can only be reached through the gate, and there’s no shortcut around it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?

The first 10 levels are free. A one-time “Full version” purchase unlocks every level. There are no ads and no subscription.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, the game plays offline. The internet is only used for purchases and optional iCloud sync.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is stored on your device. If iCloud is on, your best results sync across your devices and come back even if you reinstall the app.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap “Restore purchases” on the store screen. You need to use the same Apple ID.

Which languages are supported?

English and Turkish. The language is chosen automatically from your device settings.

Do you collect data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
